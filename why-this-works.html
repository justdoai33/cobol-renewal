Why This Works

← Prev: Methodology Next: The Deliverables →

The Breakthrough page shows this is fast. The Methodology page shows it's rigorous — EARS syntax, nine-section specs, phased gates, byte-for-byte equivalence testing. The obvious question: if it's 20x faster, what's being skipped?

Nothing. The speed and the rigor come from the same place.

  • Cheap iteration is what makes the numbers possible

    Testing a hypothesis about what a COBOL module does used to cost an afternoon of manual tracing. With an AI assistant it costs a prompt: map a call graph, simulate an input, explain a branch, get an answer in minutes. That drop in the cost of experimentation is the entire mechanism behind the 4–6 weeks → one working session comparison on the Breakthrough page. It isn't a shortcut through the process — it's the same process, run at a much lower cost per question asked.

  • The time saved gets reinvested in checking, not skipped

    Faster analysis doesn't mean fewer gates — it means the same gates (traceability, GAPS tracking, equivalence testing) get run against a fully-read system instead of a partially-read one. Byte-for-byte equivalence stays the non-negotiable bar for every component; the AI just gets you to that checkpoint in hours instead of weeks, with the gap analysis already done rather than discovered mid-implementation.

Speed here means the discipline gets applied more often, earlier, and more cheaply — not applied less.
